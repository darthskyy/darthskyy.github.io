morphparse - deep learning for morphological parsing|cael marquard & simbarashe mawere|uct cs honours|2024|https://projects.cs.uct.ac.za/honsproj/cgi-bin/view/2024/marquard_mawere.zip/index.html|This paper investigates morphological parsing for Nguni languages, comparing neural approaches (LSTMs, CRFs, and fine-tuned language models) with traditional rule-based methods. The research shows neural parsers significantly outperform rule-based baselines, with the surprising finding that models trained from scratch often outperform pretrained models, confirming the viability of neural taggers with pre-existing segmenters for these agglutinative languages.|morphparse.png
